Legend has it that there once was a young boy named Ma Liang. 
His mother and father both died early, thus leaving him on his own, only able to cut firewood and grass to make a living. 
As a youth, he already enjoyed learning to paint, though he didn’t own even a single painting brush!
One day, he walked past the entrance of a small school. 
Inside he saw the painting instructor holding a brush and painting away. 
Without thinking, he stepped inside and addressed the instructor: “I want to learn to paint. Can you lend me a brush to use?”
The teacher glared at Ma Liang. 
“Ba!” 
He hacked a mouthful of spit on Ma Liang’s face and scolded him: “A poor child wants to pick up a brush, does he? You want to learn to paint?! Hah! In your dreams!” 
When he finished, he had Ma Liang promptly thrown out the door.
